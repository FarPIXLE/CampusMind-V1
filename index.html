About CampusMind

Dedicated to supporting student mental health with evidence-based tools and compassionate care

🎯

Our Mission

To make mental health support accessible, stigma-free, and student-centered. We believe every student deserves comprehensive mental health resources.

🔒

Privacy & Security

Your journal, check-ins, and tool history are stored in this browser on this device. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so use a device you trust and clear browser data if you want to remove them.

🏆

Evidence-Based

Our tools and resources are based on clinical research and validated mental health practices, developed in consultation with licensed professionals.
